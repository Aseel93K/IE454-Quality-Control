Fix the cause, then hold the gain

A quick fix makes the symptom go away for a week. DMAIC is the five-step route that finds the real cause, proves the fix works, and keeps it working. Play with the steps below until the route feels natural.

DMAIC (typically pronounced "duh-MAY-ick") is a structured five-step problem-solving procedure. Montgomery, Chapter 2 overview

Part 3 · Activity 2 of 2 · Section 2.4 · Bottled-water filling line

Rank the risks with FMEA

Score each failure mode from 1 to 10. RPN = likelihood × detection × severity. Fix the highest RPN first.

Likelihood: 1 = not likely, 10 = almost certain. Detection: 1 = very likely to detect, 10 = very unlikely to detect. Severity: 1 = little impact, 10 = extreme impact. The starting scores are a worked example; change them and watch the ranking move.
