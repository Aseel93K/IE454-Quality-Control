Can't measure it? Count it.

A leaking can, a wrong purchase order, a scratch on a board: many quality characteristics are attributes. Count the bad units with a p or np chart, count the defects with a c or u chart, and learn how to design these charts and when a variables chart would do better.

Attributes charts are generally not as informative as variables charts, but they are very useful in service industries and transactional processes. Montgomery, Section 7.1

Defect or defective?

A nonconforming (defective) unit fails one or more specifications. A nonconformity (defect) is each single place where a specification is not met. A unit can carry several defects and still be conforming. Decide what each scene counts.

Build a c chart
